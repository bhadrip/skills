# Red Lentil Spinach Dal

A mild, tomato-forward dal that uses pre-cooked lentils for a fast finish.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked red lentils, including a little cooking liquid
- 1 1/2 cups crushed tomatoes
- 5 ounces baby spinach
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- 1 lemon
- Cooked rice or flatbread, for serving

## Weekend prep

Cook the lentils and rice. Combine cumin, coriander, turmeric, and garam masala in a small labeled container. Refrigerate what will be used within the week; freeze extra dal portions after cooling.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in ginger and the spice mix for 30 seconds.
2. Add tomatoes and simmer for 3 minutes.
3. Stir in cooked lentils. Add a splash of water if needed and heat for 5 to 7 minutes.
4. Fold in spinach until wilted. Season with salt and lemon juice.
5. Serve with reheated rice or flatbread.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze cooled dal without rice. Thaw in the refrigerator or reheat gently from frozen with a splash of water. For a vegetarian variation, finish with plain yogurt; keep the base vegan.
