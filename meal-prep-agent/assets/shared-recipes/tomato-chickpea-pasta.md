# Tomato Chickpea Pasta

A pantry-friendly tomato sauce with chickpeas for protein and body.

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces short pasta
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups crushed tomatoes
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes, optional
- 4 ounces baby spinach
- Salt and black pepper

## Weekend prep

Simmer the garlic, tomatoes, chickpeas, oregano, and pepper flakes for 15 minutes. Cool and refrigerate or freeze in meal-size portions. Keep the pasta uncooked.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
2. Heat the prepared sauce in a wide pan for 5 minutes.
3. Fold spinach into the sauce until wilted.
4. Drain pasta, reserving a splash of water. Toss with sauce and loosen as needed.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze the sauce, not the cooked pasta. Add chopped olives for a briny version or blend part of the chickpeas for a creamier sauce.
