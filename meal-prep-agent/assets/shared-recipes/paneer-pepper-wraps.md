# Paneer Pepper Wraps

Quick spiced paneer and peppers with a cool yogurt sauce.

## Ingredients

- 14 ounces paneer, cut into small cubes
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 8 small tortillas
- 3/4 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro, optional

## Weekend prep

Slice peppers and onion. Mix yogurt, lemon, cilantro, and a pinch of salt. Cube paneer no more than two days ahead, or leave sealed until cooking.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add onion and peppers; cook for 5 minutes.
3. Add paneer, garam masala, cumin, and salt. Cook, turning occasionally, for 6 to 8 minutes.
4. Warm tortillas and fill with the paneer mixture and yogurt sauce.

## Freezer and variations

Assembled wraps are best fresh. Freeze a cooked pepper-onion masala base; add fresh paneer during reheating. For a vegan version, replace paneer with extra-firm tofu and yogurt with a plant-based alternative that meets household allergen needs.
