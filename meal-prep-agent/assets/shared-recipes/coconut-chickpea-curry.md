# Coconut Chickpea Curry

A flexible curry base that works with spinach, peas, or quick-cooking vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 cup crushed tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- 2 teaspoons curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 4 ounces baby spinach
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Salt
- Cooked rice, for serving

## Weekend prep

Cook rice. Measure the spices and drain the chickpeas if cooking within two days. A complete curry batch can also be cooked, cooled, and frozen for emergency dinners.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in ginger and spices for 30 seconds.
2. Add tomatoes, coconut milk, and chickpeas. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes.
3. Fold in spinach until wilted. Season with salt and lime.
4. Serve over reheated rice.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze cooled curry in complete-dinner portions, with rice packaged separately. Add frozen peas or diced pre-roasted cauliflower for variety.
