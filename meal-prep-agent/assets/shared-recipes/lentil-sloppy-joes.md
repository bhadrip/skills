# Lentil Sloppy Joes

A tangy, lightly sweet lentil filling for buns or baked potatoes.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked brown or green lentils
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 2 teaspoons brown sugar or maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 4 burger buns
- Shredded cabbage or pickles, optional

## Weekend prep

Cook lentils. Dice the onion and mix tomato sauce, paste, soy sauce, vinegar, sweetener, and paprika.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook onion for 4 minutes.
2. Add lentils and prepared sauce. Simmer for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Toast the buns while the filling thickens.
4. Spoon filling onto buns and add cabbage or pickles.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze cooled filling in dinner-size portions. Serve over baked sweet potatoes for a wheat-free variation, using suitable tamari.
