# Spinach Mushroom Quesadillas

Savory mushroom and spinach filling folded with cheese in crisp tortillas.

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces mushrooms, sliced
- 5 ounces baby spinach
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 8 medium tortillas
- Salsa or plain yogurt, for serving
- Salt and black pepper

## Weekend prep

Slice mushrooms. If preferred, cook the complete filling ahead, cool it, and refrigerate in a shallow container.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook mushrooms for 6 minutes, until browned and their liquid has evaporated.
2. Add spinach, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Cook until the spinach wilts.
3. Divide filling and cheese among four tortillas. Top with the remaining tortillas.
4. Cook each quesadilla in a dry skillet for 2 to 3 minutes per side; cut into wedges.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze the cooked filling, not assembled quesadillas. For a vegan variation, use mashed white beans and a suitable dairy-free cheese.
