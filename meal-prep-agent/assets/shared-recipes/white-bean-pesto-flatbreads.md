# White Bean Pesto Flatbreads

Crisp flatbreads topped with creamy beans, pesto, tomatoes, and mozzarella.

## Ingredients

- 4 naan or sturdy flatbreads
- 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/3 cup prepared vegetarian pesto
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 2 cups arugula
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper

## Weekend prep

Mash the beans with 2 tablespoons pesto and a pinch of salt. Halve the tomatoes. Confirm the pesto contains no animal rennet if that matters to the household.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat the oven or toaster oven to 425°F / 220°C.
2. Spread flatbreads with the bean mixture. Top with remaining pesto, tomatoes, and mozzarella.
3. Bake for 7 to 9 minutes, until crisp and bubbling.
4. Toss arugula with oil and lemon; scatter over the flatbreads.

## Freezer and variations

Assemble fresh for the best texture. For a vegan version, use dairy-free pesto and cheese or omit the cheese and add toasted seeds.
