# Peanut Tofu Noodles

Creamy peanut noodles with browned tofu and crunchy vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces noodles
- 14 ounces extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 2 cups thinly sliced vegetables, such as bell pepper and carrot
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 1/3 cup peanut butter
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup or brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1/3 to 1/2 cup warm water

## Weekend prep

Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, lime, sweetener, ginger, and 1/3 cup water into a pourable sauce. Slice vegetables and press tofu.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Boil noodles according to the package directions. Reserve a little cooking water and drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Brown tofu for 6 to 8 minutes.
3. Add vegetables and cook for 2 minutes so they stay crisp.
4. Toss noodles, tofu, vegetables, and peanut sauce. Loosen with cooking water as needed.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze sauce separately; assembled noodles soften after freezing. Use sunflower-seed butter for a peanut-free variation and verify other allergen needs.
