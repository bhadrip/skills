# Black Bean Corn Tacos

Smoky bean-and-corn tacos with a crisp cabbage topping.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 1/2 cups corn kernels
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 cup prepared salsa
- 8 small corn or flour tortillas
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 1 lime
- 1 avocado, optional

## Weekend prep

Shred the cabbage and mix it with half the lime juice and a pinch of salt. Combine the spices. Drain the beans only if they will be cooked within two days; otherwise leave cans sealed.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add corn and cook for 3 minutes.
2. Add beans, spices, and salsa. Mash about one quarter of the beans and heat for 5 minutes.
3. Warm tortillas in a dry pan or microwave.
4. Fill with the bean mixture, cabbage, remaining lime, and avocado if using.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze cooled filling in meal-size portions. For extra heat, add chipotle; for a milder plate, serve salsa on the side.
