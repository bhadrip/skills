# Sesame Edamame Fried Rice

A fast dinner built around cold cooked rice, edamame, and pre-chopped vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups cold cooked rice
- 2 cups shelled frozen edamame
- 2 cups diced vegetables, such as carrots, peppers, and peas
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
- 2 scallions, sliced

## Weekend prep

Cook and cool the rice promptly, then refrigerate or freeze it. Dice the vegetables and mix the soy sauce, sesame oil, and vinegar.

## Weeknight cooking

1. Heat neutral oil in a wide skillet over high heat.
2. Add vegetables and edamame; stir-fry for 4 minutes.
3. Add cold rice and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, breaking up clumps.
4. Pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute. Finish with scallions.

## Freezer and variations

Freeze cooled cooked rice in flat meal-size bags. Add scrambled egg for a vegetarian version, or use baked tofu for more protein.
