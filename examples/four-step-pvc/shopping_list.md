# Shopping list — 4-padi PVC Golu display

Quantities come from the generated geometry. Prices are intentionally omitted unless current local supplier pricing is researched separately.

## PVC and fittings

- **13 × 10 ft** straight lengths of **1.5 in nominal PVC**
  - Planned finished pieces: 56 short cuts at 12 in and 19 width cuts at 36 in
  - Cut-plan allowance: 0.125 in per cut
  - Estimated total remnant after kerf: 194.625 in
- **14 × 3-port multi-axis PVC fittings**, with axes matching `connector_map.csv`
- **12 × 4-port multi-axis PVC fittings**, with axes matching `connector_map.csv`
- **12 × 5-port multi-axis PVC fittings**, with axes matching `connector_map.csv`

## Treads and retention

- **4 × tread boards**, each 40 × 13.25 × 0.75 in
- Removable board straps/clips or another suitable retention system

## Assembly and safety supplies

- Tape measure, permanent marker, square, and level
- Ratcheting PVC cutter rated for at least 1.9 in OD, or a fine-tooth saw and miter box
- Deburring/chamfering tool or flat and round files
- Rubber mallet and a scrap wood tapping block
- Safety glasses and work gloves
- Clamps or a helper for joining the two side frames
- Anti-tip restraints appropriate to the installation location
- Optional joining supplies only when approved by the selected fitting manufacturer

## Purchase checks

- Confirm the selected fittings fit 1.5 in nominal PVC with 1.9 in OD.
- Replace the generic 1 in center-to-socket-stop assumption with the chosen fitting's measured value before cutting.
- Confirm available stock is at least 120 in long and straight enough for the cuts in `cut_plan.csv`.
- Confirm the intended installation can accommodate the modeled 58.56 × 40.56 × 59.41 in envelope.
- Obtain independent structural and safety review for the intended load and environment.
